# Code of conduct

This project uses the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the code. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not okay. Don't post anyone's personal information, including other students' names, posts or grades from Moodle or Ed.

If something happens, contact the maintainer privately at wensx0926@gmail.com. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, issues or pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who keep doing it.
